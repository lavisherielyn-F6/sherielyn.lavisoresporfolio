About

I earned a BS in Management Accounting from Liceo de Cagayan University (2022), started in inventory control, moved into accounts payable in 2024, and since April 2025 have worked remotely as bookkeeper and virtual assistant for FierceSix Inc. (F6) and WRTX Management LLC, a Texas based healthcare and wellness group.

I work daily with a US team across bookkeeping, payables, payroll support, vendor reconciliation and documentation. I also run the admin side: email, calendar scheduling, vendor follow ups and document preparation. I am Civil Service eligible (Career Service Professional, BCLTE), detail oriented, confidential, and comfortable owning a reconciliation from raw statement to finished report.
